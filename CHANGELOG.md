# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## \[1.0.0]

Original version. I used this to build myself an internal tool as well, you could do the same; there is no organisation-specific content. Includes the article editor, 20
templates, wikitext export and import, Word and email import, an offline
house-rule checker, and publishing to a MediaWiki site with your own bot
password.


Note that this was built to work on Media Wiki 2017.
