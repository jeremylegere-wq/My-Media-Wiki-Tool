Writing on the page

  • Click anywhere on the page and type. It already looks the way it will on the wiki.
  • Grey italic text is a prompt. Click it and type to replace it. Prompts you leave are not shown on the wiki: they become hidden TODO notes, and Check lists them.
  • Hover over a block and press + to add a block below it, or type / on an empty line.
  • Select a block to see its tools: colours, move, copy, delete, and extras like add row.
  • Drag a block by its ⋮⋮ handle to move it.

Keyboard

Ctrl+KAdd a link
Ctrl+EInline code
/Insert a block (on an empty line)
Ctrl+EnterAdd another block like this one
Alt+↑ / Alt+↓Move the block up or down
TabNext table cell (adds a row at the end)
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo, redo
Ctrl+SSave a draft

Opening and publishing on the wiki

  • Press Wiki to connect with your own bot password (YourWikiName@BotName, made at Special:BotPasswords). The tool uses it to log in and never saves it.
  • Open, then A page on the wiki, to edit a page that is already there.
  • Publish shows exactly what will change and what Check found, then saves the page under your name. A copy of the old version goes in output\wiki_backups.
  • If someone else changed the page after you opened it, the wiki refuses the save and the tool asks what to do.

Rules the export keeps for you

  • Hidden first line with the title and summary.
  • Contents box, house colours, headings and numbered steps.
  • No image widths. Pictures link to a full-size copy.
  • Check warns about passwords, sharing links, email addresses and phone numbers.
